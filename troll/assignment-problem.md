## Question

How do you find a minimum/maximum solution to the random assignment problem (assignment problem, programming)?

## Content

I assume you are asking for the [assignment problem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Assignment_problem).

The solution to the assignment problem is the [Hungarian algorithm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hungarian_algorithm).

Check [this](https://cse.ust.hk/~golin/COMP572/Notes/Matching.pdf) out for the $O(n^3)$ algorithm.
